# Donate Bangladesh
A Platform to Make Giving Simple and Transparent
Donate Bangladesh is a user-friendly platform designed to make donations seamless while ensuring transparency in the funding process. Whether you're looking to contribute to a cause or track how much you’ve donated, this site provides all the features to keep donors informed and empowered.


# 🌟 Key Features

- **Real-time Funding Updates:** Every time a donation is made, the total funds raised are updated instantly. You can see the collective impact your donations are making in real time.

- **Track Your Transactions:** Easily access your personal donation history. Stay in control and keep track of all your contributions to various causes.

- **Seamless Donation Process:** The platform makes donating easy. When you decide to contribute, a donation modal will open, allowing for secure and fast transactions.

- **Interactive Balance Management:** Upon completing a donation, your current balance decreases, while the total donation fund increases automatically, giving you a clear picture of your financial impact.

- **Where Your Donations Go:** Each donation reflects where the funds were directed, providing transparency and building trust in the process.


# 💻 Tech Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML5,Tailwind CSS, JavaScript (DOM Manipulations)
- **Modal & Payment Handling:** JavaScript
- **Data Handling:** Real-time balance and fund management


# 🚀 How It Works
- **Donation Process:** Users can choose to donate from their current balance by selecting a cause.
- **Modal for Payment:** Once the donation is initiated, a modal pops up, allowing users to complete the payment securely.
- **Transaction Update:** Upon successful donation, the current balance decreases and the total raised funds increase.
- **View Transaction History:** Donors can view their complete transaction history, ensuring transparency and accountability.

# 📊 Real-Time Fundraising
The website features live updates for both your personal balance and the total funds raised, showing how each contribution impacts the bigger picture.

# 🌍 Empowering Change in Bangladesh
Donate Bangladesh is committed to making it easier for people to support causes that matter. Every donation counts, and with real-time tracking, you can see exactly how your contributions are making a difference!
